# How to Submit an Expense Reimbursement

## Steps
1. Keep the original receipt for any business expense over ₹200.
2. Fill out the expense form (available from the Finance intern, Apoorva Nair).
3. Attach a scanned or photographed copy of the receipt.
4. Submit the form to finance@laratech.com within 15 days of the expense.

## What's reimbursable
- Local travel for client/company meetings
- Work-related software subscriptions (with manager approval)
- Meals during approved overtime or travel

## What's NOT reimbursable
- Personal purchases
- Commute between home and office
- Expenses submitted more than 30 days after the purchase date

## Processing time
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.
