# New Intern Onboarding Guide

## Day 1 checklist
1. Collect your laptop and access badge from IT Support (Rakshitha Shetty, IT department).
2. Set up your company email account (firstname@laratech.com).
3. Join the #interns channel on the internal chat tool.
4. Complete the mandatory security awareness training within your first 3 days.

## First week
- Meet your assigned mentor for a 1:1 kickoff.
- Set up your development environment following your team's setup guide.
- Review the company policies (available in the AI Knowledge Hub under your folder).

## Getting help
If you're stuck, post in #interns or reach out to your mentor directly. For IT issues
(laptop, VPN, account access), contact IT Support.
