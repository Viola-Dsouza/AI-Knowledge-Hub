# IT Support FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to the internal SSO portal and click "Forgot password." If you're locked out
entirely, contact IT Support directly.

## How do I connect to the VPN?
Install the company VPN client from the internal software catalog, then log in
with your company email and SSO password. Contact IT Support if you don't have
access to the catalog yet.

## My laptop won't turn on. What do I do?
1. Confirm it's charged (plug it in for 15 minutes).
2. Try a hard reset (hold the power button for 10 seconds).
3. If it still won't boot, contact IT Support to arrange a replacement or repair.

## How do I request new software?
Submit a request through the internal IT ticketing system with a business
justification. Most requests are reviewed within 2 business days.
